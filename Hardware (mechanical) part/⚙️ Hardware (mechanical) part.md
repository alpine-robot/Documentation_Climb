## Current mechanical configuration

The physical ALPINE body is rope-supported by two winches and uses a pneumatic leg for jump initiation. The 2026 integrated configuration adds six fixed-axis electric ducted fans (EDFs), structural supports, ESC protection and two onboard battery packs.

The complete integrated body is approximately **10 kg**.

![Integrated ALPINE body](../0_Images/Andrea_Dalla_Villa_2026/fig2_1_integrated_body_CAD.png)

The six-EDF system is described in [[EDF attitude-control system]].
