# Orientation Control and Experimental Integration of the ALPINE Climbing Robot

**Author:** Andrea Dalla Villa  
**Supervisor:** prof. Michele Focchi  
**University:** University of Trento - Department of Information Engineering and Computer Science  
**Degree:** Bachelor's Degree in Computer, Communications and Electronic Engineering  
**Academic year:** 2025/2026

## Scope

The dissertation documents the 2026 experimental integration of the physical ALPINE platform.

Main contributions:
- design and integration of a six-EDF attitude-control subsystem;
- onboard pitch and yaw feedback control;
- EDF thrust-command characterisation;
- pneumatic command characterisation;
- repeatable winch homing;
- homing-relative rope-position feedback;
- body-pose reconstruction;
- coordinated real-robot jump execution;
- functional validation of subsystem compatibility.

## Final validation status

The final experiment established a repeatable real-robot sequence from bring-up and homing to pre-jump positioning, user-triggered jump and suspended post-jump hold while attitude regulation remained active.

Quantitative trajectory validation, direct rope-tension measurement, automatic touchdown detection and planner-driven consecutive physical jumps remain future work.
